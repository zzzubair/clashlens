# Player dashboard design

Settled with the owner on **2026-10-12**. This is the design for the signed-in player dashboard. All 13 cards are built. The data each card needs, and whether Clash Lens already has it, is in [dashboard-data.md](dashboard-data.md).

The dashboard can be built behind the off switch, but nothing goes live until tracking passes the accuracy bar.

## How the page works

- **One page, no tabs.** Big account tabs at the top (name, live trophies, rank, net today) with a "+ Link player" button after them; the cards follow the selected account. One layout for all of a user's accounts. To the right of the tabs is a toolbar: "Updated N min ago" (only when the Legend day card, which says it itself, is not on the dashboard), "Player page" (opens the selected account's page) and "⚙ Customise". A phone folds the toolbar into one icon button.
- **Account not in Legend I:** selecting it replaces the cards with a large message, "Unfortunately, the dashboard is Legend I only. Support for lower leagues is coming in the future!", and hides Customise.
- **Grid:** 3 columns. Small = 1 column, Medium = 2, Large = a full row. Cards never scroll inside: each grows to fit its content, and the cards in a row match the tallest one. On phones every card is full width, one under another, and height follows content. iPads and small laptops use 2 columns, where Small takes half, Medium the whole row and each card keeps its own height. When the next card doesn't fit the room left in a row, the first later card that fits moves up into it, never from past a Large card; with none, the row's last card widens, so no row has a hole. While customising, cards show in their saved order. A card that moves up keeps its saved place in keyboard and screen-reader order, so Tab reaches it after the cards shown below it. The default layout moves no card; a custom layout with a gap can move one on any wide screen.
- **Live or not:** "Updated N min ago" shows once for the page: in the top-right corner of the Legend day card, or, when that card is not on the dashboard, in the toolbar (from the first of Legend clock, Rivals watch or Cutoffs). A card without live data shows finished Legend days and says "Last 7 days" / "Last 3 days", never "finished days". Estimates say so: "Projected place", and "estimate" read aloud. Ranks are just "Rank".
- **Controls:** a choice you flip while looking is a switch on the card (3 days / 7 days / Season, Trophies / Rank, Live / Season, Net / Offense / Defense) or, for Watchlist, a watchlist picker on the card and, for Goal, a "Plan: skip N days" menu in its header. A set-once choice is a card setting in Customise.
- **Customise:** a bar names the layout ("Default layout" or "Your own layout") and offers "+ Add a card" (each card listed with its size and one-line description), "Use default layout" (greyed out while already on it), Cancel (or Escape, which discards the changes) and Done. Each card gets a strip with its drag handle, name, ↑ ↓ and a trash icon (remove); cards with settings also show a "⚙ Settings" button in the strip (just the gear on a phone). On a phone the bar is fixed to the bottom of the screen and replaces the tab bar until Done.
- **Default layout:** each user is either on the default layout or on their own. Users on the default get the new default when it ships, including new cards. Any move, remove or add switches them to their own layout, which we never change for them; "Use default layout" puts them back.
- **First visit:** a pop-up offers "Use our layout" (recommended) or "Build my own" (starts empty with the card list open), with "Change it any time with ⚙ Customise dashboard".
- **Watchlists, not saved players.** Watchlist cards show the watchlist's real members plus the selected account (on the viewer's card only). Other linked accounts appear only if they are members; accounts not in Legend I never appear.
- **Names:** long, emoji, symbol or right-to-left names are cut with "…" and shown in full on hover.

**Goal skip plan:** days you plan to sit out under a shield count as 0 for you while the goal line keeps moving, so trophies a day needed = (gap + line speed × days left) ÷ (days left − skipped days). A "Plan: skip N days ▾" menu in the card's header starts at 0 and opens a line ("Days you'll sit out under a shield; the rest make up the need.") over a 0 / 1 / 2 / 3 switch; picking a length changes the Need bar and the On pace / Behind verdict, shades that many days at the end of the chart as "Skip", and closes the menu. There is no menu while the card says it needs one full day (Day 1), and a length that would cover every remaining day is not offered. Legend I has only one-day and two-day shields; they can be stacked, and their waits before rebuying mean three days in a row is the longest possible.

## Dropped, and why

- Head-to-head and Personal bests: the owner didn't like them. Trophy trend: Recent form covers it.
- Nemesis: one account hits the same account again only rarely (99.7% of attacker-defender pairs in a Season happen once).
- Ghost race: too close to Goal and Rivals watch.
- Attack stats / Defense stats: replaced by Offense consistency, Recent form and the two army cards.
- Medium Armies on offense: most players use one army type (66% one, 22% two), so the Small card won.
- Average stars on Armies on defense: 2.6 against 2.4 stars is still a two-star attack.
- Separate Offense and Defense heat map cards: one card with a switch instead.

## Open items

1. **Next Reset rank:** the outer range and one projected place inside it are shown. No narrower range is shown: tests found a useful-width range only for players who had finished their battles and the Top 200.
2. **Heat map colours compare you with yourself:** a good day can show red if it is your worst, and with only 2–3 days the colours swing between extremes.
3. **Legends trophies lost:** −32 a defense across all Legends was steady over 13 days (29 Sep–11 Oct), but it rises with trophies, so Trophies mode compares each base with Legends at similar trophies, worked out live from today's defenses like the held rate (about 52% over those days across all Legends, lower at higher trophies).
4. **Not tested:** dragging cards with a real finger on phone or iPad (the ↑ ↓ arrows always work).
5. **Build checks:** Goal's dashed goal line must stay visible in dark mode; Goal's "Behind by N/day" label must fit for far goals (Top 1–50 from rank 318 overflowed in the design draft); real defenses cost about 32 trophies each (a full 8-defense day about −260), more than some draft numbers.
6. **Unused Trophy trend code (owner decision):** `website/app/components/PlayerTrends.tsx` is imported by nothing but its own test and is kept for a Trophy trend card this design dropped. Deleting it waits for the owner's approval.
7. **Share a card as an image or link:** a later idea. Clash Lens has no card image maker yet, so one would be new work.
8. **Custom defense window across Seasons (owner decision):** Armies on defense can only use days still kept. A finished Season's daily logs and army battle records are deleted 7 days after it ends, so early in a new Season a long Custom window covers fewer days. Covering the full window would need saved per-player defense totals or longer retention.
9. **Ideas not built:** Field meta movers, Peer comparison, My accounts, Shield tomorrow?, Crew cup, Participation calendar, Results needing caution. Needing equipment-level army types or new collection: Balance-aware comparison, Training review queue, Equipment-pair watch, castle-and-siege toolbox, garrison diary. Base notebook on hold.
